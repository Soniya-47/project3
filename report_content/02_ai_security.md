# Chapter 2: Deep Dive - AI & Autonomous Systems Security (2025-2035)

## The Rise of Agentic AI

The next frontier of Artificial Intelligence is *Agentic AI*—autonomous systems capable of perceiving their environment, reasoning, creating plans, and executing actions to achieve goals with minimal human intervention. By 2030, Agentic AI will dominate enterprise workflows, from automated coding assistants to autonomous supply chain managers. This shift introduces a profound new attack surface: **AI-on-AI Warfare**.

Traditional cybersecurity focused on static defenses (firewalls, endpoint protection). In the era of Agentic AI, security becomes dynamic. Adversaries will deploy *offensive AI agents* designed to probe defenses 24/7, identify zero-day vulnerabilities in milliseconds, and launch coordinated attacks at machine speed. Defenders must counter with *defensive AI agents* capable of autonomous threat hunting and real-time remediation.

## Adversarial Machine Learning: Poisoning & Evasion

As AI models become critical infrastructure, they become high-value targets. **Adversarial Machine Learning (AML)** is the study of attacks on machine learning algorithms.

*   **Data Poisoning:** Attackers compromise the training data of an AI model to introduce backdoors or biases. For example, subtly altering a stop sign in a training dataset so that an autonomous vehicle categorizes it as a speed limit sign.
*   **Model Evasion:** Crafting inputs (adversarial examples) that are imperceptible to humans but cause the AI to malfunction. Evasion attacks can bypass facial recognition systems, spam filters, or malware detectors.
*   **Model Inversion & Extraction:** Reconstructing sensitive training data (e.g., patient records) from the model's outputs or stealing the proprietary model itself.

By 2035, securing the *AI Supply Chain*—from data collection to model deployment—will be as critical as securing the software supply chain is today.

## Securing Autonomous Fleets

The physical manifestation of AI is the autonomous system: self-driving cars, delivery drones, and industrial robotics. The cybersecurity of these systems is a matter of life and death.

*   **Autonomous Vehicles (AVs):** A compromised AV fleet could be weaponized to cause gridlock or mass casualties. Security must go beyond the infotainment system to the Controller Area Network (CAN) bus and the AI decision-making unit.
*   **Drones & UAVs:** As delivery drones fill the skies, jamming and spoofing attacks (GPS denial) will become commonplace. "Drone-jacking" will be a new form of theft.
*   **Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT):** Smart factories rely on interconnected robots. A cyberattack on an IIoT network could sabotage production lines, cause physical damage to equipment, or endanger workers.

## Market Projections & Key Players

The global AI in Cybersecurity market is projected to reach **$93.75 billion by 2030**, growing at a CAGR of over 20%.

**Key Emerging Players and Technologies:**
*   **Darktrace & SentinelOne:** Leaders in AI-driven threat detection and response.
*   **HiddenLayer & Protect AI:** Pioneers in ML Security (MLSecOps), focusing on securing the AI models themselves.
*   **Robust Intelligence:** Specializing in AI risk management and model validation.

The demand for **AI Security Architects** and **Adversarial AI Testers** will far outstrip supply, making these some of the most lucrative roles in the industry.
